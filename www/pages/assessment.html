Assessment Overview

Instructions

The purpose of this assessment is to evaluate your understanding and application of the concepts covered in the in this course. You will be required to complete a series of tasks that simulate real-world engineering challenges related to operational technology (OT) systems and Cyber-Informed Engineering principles.

Task 1: Component Identification

Identify and list all components associated with Operational Technology (OT).

Status: Not Started

Task 2: Threat Intelligence

Identify potential threats and vulnerabilities in the OT system.

Status: Not Started

Task 3: Engineering Controls

Identify and describe five (5) engineering controls or design modifications that could be implemented to mitigate the risk of equipment destruction or hazards to personnel resulting from cyber-attacks. Consider both physical and cyber controls that align with industry best practices.

Status: Not Started

Task 4: Supply Chain Security

Identify and assess risks associated with the supply chain.

Status: Not Started

Report Requirements

You will be required to hand in a comprehensive report. The requirements for this report are detailed in each task below. Ensure the report is formatted professionally and includes all necessary sections as specified.

Report Format:

  • Title Page: Include the assessment title, your name, and the date
  • Table of Contents: List all sections and their page numbers
  • Executive Summary: Summarize the key points of the report (300-400 words)
  • Tasks: List each task by their task number and title
    • Task 1: Component Identification
    • Task 2: Threat Intelligence
    • Task 3: Engineering Controls
    • Task 4: Supply Chain Security
  • Top 10 Mitigation Strategies: List the top 10 mitigation strategies identified during the assessment
  • References: List all sources and references used in the report
  • Acknowledgements: If using AI, acknowledge the tools and resources utilized
  • Appendices: Include if necessary
