Frequently Asked Questions

What are the system requirements?

Your workstation needs: OpenPLC Runtime, OpenPLC Editor, Ignition, and Ignition Designer installed. A modern web browser is required for this platform.

How do I submit my assessment?

Navigate to the Assessment page and complete all required tasks. Use the submission form to submit your work by the deadline.

Where can I get help?

Contact your instructor via email at engineering@ualr.edu or visit the Support page for troubleshooting guides.

Can I work with others?

Check your course syllabus for collaboration policies. Individual work is typically required unless otherwise specified by your instructor.
