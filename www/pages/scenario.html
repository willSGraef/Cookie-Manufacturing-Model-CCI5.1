Resource Allocation

Silo System for Cookie Production Line

Golden Bake™ Integrated Dough Dosing and Mixing System is the critical starting point for any high-performance cookie or biscuit production line, engineered to ensure uncompromising quality and resource efficiency. The system automates the allocation and combining of all raw materials into a consistent dough batch.

Bulk ingredients, specifically Flour and Sugar, are efficiently drawn from external storage silos and automatically conveyed to our high-level Material Receiving Station. This station utilizes Loss-in-Weight precision technology to guarantee the accurate, gravimetric metering of these powdered resources, critical for minimizing material waste and ensuring recipe uniformity. Water is precisely metered through a dedicated piping system and introduced into the robust Industrial Mixer.

The core of our system is the mixer itself, which is designed for speed and quality. It achieves a superior coefficient of variation (CV) value of ≥95% in just 120 seconds, demonstrating rapid and near-perfect ingredient distribution. Furthermore, the design ensures less than 0.1% residue after mixing, maximizing yield and minimizing waste between batches. To ensure the integrity of your product, the mixer causes no damage to the product structure, maintaining the desired texture. The mixer features a maximum 60° closed large opening, allowing for both thorough mixing and rapid unloading in the shortest possible time.

The entire process is managed via an intuitive HMI screen, allowing the operator to oversee the automated dosing and manage the manual introduction of specialized minor ingredients. This blend of precision automation and controlled labor ensures that every batch leaves the mixing area perfectly prepared, delivering the highest quality dough with optimized resource and time usage.

Production Area

Forming, Cutting & Freezing

The Production Area transforms the prepared dough into frozen, retail-ready cookies through a highly efficient, two-stage process. The journey begins with the dough batch entering the Baker Perkins TruClean™ Wirecut, a machine engineered to handle soft, sticky, or chunky doughs and capable of producing cookies ranging from 1.5″ (38mm) to 4.8″ (121mm) in size, with an impressive output of up to 200 rows per minute. After the dough is extruded and formed, the system's quiet, reliable servo-driven paper cutter precisely slices the supporting paper sheet to maximize product patterning and ensure efficient use of freezer space. Immediately following forming, the cookies enter the Linde Cryovantage Tunnel Freezer for rapid flash-freezing. This cryogenic system relies on precise resource allocation, primarily liquid Nitrogen (LN 2), controlled via a main gas valve (GV 1) to rapidly drop the product temperature to optimal levels for freezing. To ensure consistent freezing quality, the tunnel incorporates three independent fan sections (Fan 1, Fan 2, Fan 3) that regulate air circulation and manage temperature distribution within the freezing chamber. Once the cookies exit the freezer fully frozen, they smoothly transition into the Finished Goods Area, completing the primary production phase and making the product ready for subsequent packing and storage.
